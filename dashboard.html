Frequently Asked Questions

What is CareerFlix? +
CareerFlix is an interactive, story-driven platform that lets you experience what different careers are really like - from a doctor's first night shift to a pilot flying through a storm - by making choices inside 5-episode stories and seeing how they play out.
Is CareerFlix free to use? +
Yes. CareerFlix is completely free to explore - there's no subscription, payment, or sign-up fee required to play any story.
Where can I use CareerFlix? +
CareerFlix runs right in your browser on any phone, tablet, or computer - no download or installation needed.
How does a story work? +
Every story has 3 to 5 episodes. You play as the main character, make decisions at key moments, and see how each choice changes the scene - just like a real day in that job.
Is CareerFlix good for students choosing a career? +
Yes - it's built for exactly that. Instead of just reading a job description, students get to "live" a realistic day in each career and see the real dilemmas and decisions that come with it.
